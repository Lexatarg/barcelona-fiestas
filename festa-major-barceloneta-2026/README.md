# Festa Major de la Barceloneta 2026

An unofficial, hour-by-hour guide to the Barceloneta neighborhood festival
in Barcelona, September 25 – October 4, 2026.
Available in Catalan, Castilian and English.

## About

A single self-contained HTML page. No build step, no dependencies, no tracking.
Each day has a timeline with times, places and directions links, followed by a
list of venues and the practical information from the official program.

## Source

All information on this page was gathered from the
[official program](https://pro.gestornoticies.dtibcn.cat/gestornoticies-pro/media/Programa_FM_Barceloneta.a7bf2a66.pdf)
of the Festa Major de la Barceloneta 2026, published by Barcelona City Council.
Please check it for the full program and any changes.

This is a personal project, made so that everyone can find the times and places
more easily and enjoy the festival. It is not an official publication.
