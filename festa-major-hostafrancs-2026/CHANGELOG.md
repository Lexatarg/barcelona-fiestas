## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-02

### Added
- First version: every day from October 2 to 11, with times, places, Directions
  and Add to calendar for each event, in Catalan, Castilian and English.
- The rain changes of October 3 and 4: canceled events are left out and listed
  under Places; moved and postponed events show their new place or time.
- Where the official sources differ, the event follows the most recent
  information and notes the other version.
