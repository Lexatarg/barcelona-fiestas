# Festa Major d'Hostafrancs 2026

An unofficial, hour-by-hour guide to the 167th Festa Major d'Hostafrancs in
Barcelona, October 2–11, 2026.
Available in Catalan, Castilian and English.

## About

A single self-contained HTML page. No build step, no dependencies, no tracking.
Each day has a timeline with times, places, directions and calendar links,
followed by a list of venues and the practical information from the official
program.

## Source

All information on this page was gathered from the
[official program](https://federaciohostafrancs.cat/filemanager/media-resource/download/279)
published by the Federació d'Hostafrancs, the federation's
[program web app](https://programafm.federaciohostafrancs.cat/) and the
Sants-Montjuïc district's
[updated program](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/sants-montjuic/ca/noticies/ja-es-festa-major-a-hostafrancs-41414)
with the rain changes. Where they differ, the guide follows the most recent
information and notes the other version on the event.

This is a personal project, made so that everyone can enjoy the festivities more
easily. It is not an official publication.
