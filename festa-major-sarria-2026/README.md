# Festa Major de Sarrià 2026

An unofficial, hour-by-hour guide to the Festa Major de Sarrià, the Festes del
Roser, in Barcelona, 2–12 October 2026.
Available in Catalan, Castilian and English.

## About

A single self-contained HTML page. No build step, no dependencies, no tracking.
Each day has a timeline with times, places and directions links, followed by a
list of venues and the practical information from the official programme.

## Source

All information on this page was gathered from the
[official programme](https://pro.gestornoticies.dtibcn.cat/gestornoticies-pro/media/Programa_Festa_Major_de_Sarria_2026.cc5fd7b5.pdf)
of the Festa Major de Sarrià 2026 and the Sarrià – Sant Gervasi district's notice
on the closure of Passatge de Mallofré. Please check the programme for the full
details and any changes.

This is a personal project, made so that everyone can enjoy the festivities more
easily. It is not an official publication.
